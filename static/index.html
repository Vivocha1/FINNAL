Money skills

Short, practical lessons to make everyday financial decisions easier.

Start with an emergency fund · 5 min

A small reserve can help handle unexpected costs without relying on expensive borrowing. Start with a realistic milestone, then work toward one month of essential expenses.

Build a budget that fits real life · 4 min

Separate essentials, flexible spending and savings. Use your transaction history to set category limits, review them after payday and adjust them when circumstances change.

Understand the full cost of credit · 6 min

Compare the annualised interest rate, processing fees, repayment schedule and late-payment consequences. Do not borrow based only on the amount offered or the monthly instalment.
